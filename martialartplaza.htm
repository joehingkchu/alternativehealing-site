Sifu

The Academy of Shaolin System is a traditional martial arts school specializing in Kung Fu, Chinese Jujitsu, Tai Chi Chuan and Qigong.  Established in 1967 in Redwood City by Sifu Joe Hing Kwok Chu and in continuous operation ever since, we are the oldest school on the Peninsula.  Throughout the years we have sponsored and participated in many mix martial arts full contact tournaments in the Agrodome in Vancouver, B.C., Madison Square Garden in New York City, and various locations in the San Francisco area.
