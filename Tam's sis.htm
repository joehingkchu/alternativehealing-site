川芎    二錢﹐  甘草  二錢﹐

 

當歸  三錢﹐ 艾葉 二錢﹐白芍 四錢﹐

 

生地黃 六錢。阿膠  二錢(另包沖服)

 

加水兩碗半煎八分。飯前溫服。

 

Add 2 and half bowls of water.

 

Simmer all herbs (except the small package of gelatin).

Simmer down to 80% of a bowl (serving size). 

Strain and pour into a warm bowl with the gelatin powder in the bowl.

Cover for 10 minutes. Use while warm.

Drink before meals.

 

 

 

 

Consult your medical doctor about using any herbs.
