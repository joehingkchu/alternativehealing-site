Guan zhi zai pu sha -- xing sheng pranya paramita shi, zhao jian wu yun jie kong.

Avolaketasvara Boddisattva(quanyin). While studying pranya paramita, discovered that the five skandas are empty.

 

She Li Zi, se ji shi kong. Kong ji shi se. Shou, xiang, xing, she yi fu ru shi.

Shariputera, form is empty and empty is form. So as perception, thinking, action, and consciousness.
