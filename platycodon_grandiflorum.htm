jie geng

by: Joe Hing Kwok Chu

桔 梗　

 Dry root of Platycodon grandiflorum (Jacq.) A.D.C本 品 為 枯 梗 科 植 物 桔 梗. 的 乾 燥 根

Pharmaceutical name:Radix Platycodi grandiflori
Biological name:Platycodon grandiflorum(Jacq.)A.DC.
Pron. in Japanese:kikyo
Pron. in Korean:kilgyong
Pron. in Cantonese:kit gang
Common Name:root of balloon flower
Distribution: 
Properties (characteristics):藥性:bitter, acrid neutral

味苦、辛，性平

Channels (meridians) entered:

歸經:

lung

肺

Actions & Indications:

rids of phlegm, inhibits cough, treats bronchitis, rids of puss, for tonsilitis, and sore throat.

可袪痰、鎮咳，治療支氣管炎、排膿、化膿性疾病、扁桃線炎、咽喉痛。有鬆痰排膿之效。

Medical Function:

藥理:

(1) expels phlegm and inhibits cough

(2) anti-inflammatory

(3) anti ulcers

(4) lower blood sugar

(l)袪痰鎮咳；(2)抗炎、抗潰瘍；(3)降血糖。

Chemical ingredients:

化學成分

　

　

　

　

　

　

　

　

　

　

　

　

　

　

　

The root contains many saponins. Ingredients are separated with hydrolysis process: platycodigenin (main saponin), small amount of polygalacic acidplatycogenic acid A, platycogenic acid BC, platycogenic acid C. From saponins the following are separated: prosapogenin, 3-O-β-glucose, platycodoside C, platycogenin-3-O-β-glucoside platycodigenin, glucose, xylose. etc

 , 根含多种皂貳，水解後分得 ：桔梗皂貳元（platycodigenin）,為主要的皂貳元，以及少量的新遠志酸（polygalacic acid）、桔梗酸A（platycogenic acid A）、桔梗酸B（platycogenic acid B）、桔梗酸C（platycogenic acid C）。此外，從皂貳中尚分得前皂貳元（prosapogenin）,為次級　，是桔梗皂貳元-3-O-β-葡萄糖貳(platycogenin-3-O-β-glucoside)　。由總皂中分得桔梗皂貳C（platycodoside C）,水解生成桔梗皂貳元（platycodigenin）和葡萄糖、木糖、鼠李糖、阿拉伯糖（2:1:1:1）。以前報道的桔梗皂貳　（platycidin）經証明為一混合貳　。根另含α-菠菜甾醇、α-菠菜甾醇-β-D-葡萄糖　、樺皮醇（betulin）、桔梗聚果糖（platycodinin）。根中尚含14种氨基酸，總含量達1.22%。
 

Dosage:

用量:

3 to 10 g

3～10克

Samples of formulae:

常用處方：

jie geng tang, jie geng bai san

桔梗湯、桔梗白散。

 

Modern Research:to be loaded
Cautions:

禁忌:

Not to use in case of yinxu (yin deficient)  with chronic cough, breathing difficulty and vomiting blood.

陰虛久嗽，氣逆及咳血者忌服。

別名: 津梗、符蔰、白藥、利如、梗草、盧如、房圖、薺苨、苦梗、苦桔梗、大藥、包袱花、四葉菜、沙油菜、 山鈴鐺花。
