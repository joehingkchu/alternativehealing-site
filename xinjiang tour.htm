WHERE TO STAY

Eden Hotel (86-998-266-4444 or 86-998-266-5555; 148 Seman Road). A newly built, Uighur-owned hotel with clean, Internet-connected rooms and a very good restaurant right in the center of Kashgar and within walking distance of the old Uighur neighborhood. Rooms are listed at 280 renminbi, or $43 at 6.5 renminbi to the dollar, but sometimes go for around 180 renminbi.

Yarkand Hotel (86-998-851-2365; 4 XinCheng Road, Yarkand). This basic hotel is centrally located and a 10-minute walk from the old town and royal tombs. Rooms for 280 renminbi.

Khotan Taxna Hotel (86-903-613-0388; 139 Beijing Donglu Road, Khotan). A Uighur-owned hotel in walking distance of mosques, the grand bazaar, jade market and restaurants. Rooms are 280 renminbi.
