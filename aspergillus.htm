Aspergillus

Aspergillus flavus and Aspergillus oryzae look very similar to each other and their DNA almost are 100% similar. They are both belong to the group aspergillus section flavi.

Aspergillus flavus produces cancer causing agent, aflatoxin (黃麴毒素), which is the strongest cancer causing agent discovered to date. Eating food contaminated with the aflatoxin creates a high probability of getting liver cancer. Aspergillus oryzae is a traditional fermenting agent used in soy sauce, wine and other food produced with fermenting process. It had been difficult to diferentiate A. flavus from A. oryzae using the physical characteristic of the fungi before the introduce of AFLP (Amplified Fragment Length Polymorphism ) method, invented by KeyGene, a biotech company based in Wageningen, Holland. The technique is now merchandised under license agreement by Perkin Elmer.
