Anti-depression Drugs      

Edited by: Joe Hing Kwok Chu

The following drugs can cause erection difficulty, loss of libido, and ejaculatory difficulty. Doxepin and phenelzimuml also cause priapism (persistent erection).

desipraminum

   Brand names: Norpramin, Pertofrane

 

doxepin

   Brand names: Adapin, Sinequan

 

impramine

   Brand names: Janimine, Imavate, Trofranil

 

nortipyline

   Brand names: Aventyl, Pamelor

 

protripyline

   Brand name: Vivactil

 

isocarboxazidum

   Brand name: Marplan

 

phenelzimun

   Brand name: Nardil

 

tranylcypromine

   Brand name: Parnate
