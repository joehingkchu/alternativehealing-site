Liu Wei Di Huang Wan

by: Joe Hing Kwok Chu

Prescription:

shu di huang 32%, shan yu rou 16%, shan yao 16%, mu dan pi 12%, fu ling 12%, ze xie 12%.  Grind into powder and make into pills.

處方﹕

熟地黃 32%、山萸肉 16%、淮山藥 16%、牡丹皮 12%、

白茯苓 12%、澤瀉 12%。 研作細末為丸。
