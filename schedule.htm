Schedule

The school is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., on Saturday and Sunday from 9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.

Kung Fu/Jujitsu

Group classes are held three times a week - Monday, Wednesday and Friday - from 7:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m.

Children from 5 - 12 years of age have class from 7:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.

Mondays and Fridays are for Kung Fu.  Wednesdays are for Chinese Jujitsu.

Visitors are welcome to watch.

Fees

  • Free uniform is offered with every ten month program.
