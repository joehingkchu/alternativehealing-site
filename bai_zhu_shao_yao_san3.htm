dang gui shao yao san 當歸芍藥散

by: Joe Hing kwok Chu

Usage: Tension in the abdomen, mild and

continuous pain during pregnancy. Irregular

menses, dizziness, fatigue, pain in lower back

and abdomen area, red and white vagina discharge . 

Promote blood and stabilizing fetus. Can be used

before and after child delivery for tonic. Also can

be used in habitual miscarriage.

Prescription:

dang gui 3 qian, bai shao   4 qian, fu ling 4 qian,

bai zhu 4 qian, ze xie  4 qian, chuan xiong 3 qian.

Boil with water and use as tea.

　

Note: qian = 3.75 g (old measurement)

　

當歸芍藥散 <金匱要略 >
　

婦人妊娠，腹中拘急，綿綿作痛。及月經不順，

頭暈目眩，疲勞倦怠，腹痛腰酸，或赤白帶下證。

養血安胎，用於產前產後之補養或妊娠腹痛，或

習慣流產者。


處 方 :

當 歸 三錢﹐白 芍 四錢﹐茯 苓 四錢﹐白 朮 四錢﹐

澤 瀉 四錢﹐川 芎三錢。水 煎 服 。

　

註﹕一錢 = 3.75 克 (舊制)
