Li Dong Yuan qing feng san

Edited by: Joe Hing Kwok Chu

　

Name of formula: qing fen san

　

Prescription:

chai hu 7.5 g, chuan xiong 5.5 g,  fang feng,  jiang huo, huang qin (stir fry with rice wine),jiang ban xia, fu ling,  dang gui,  dan pi, dan shen,  11 g  of each, wu zhu yu 4 g, ze xie,  chuan niu xi, chi shao,  15 g of each,  huai niu xi 30 g. Boil with water. One prescription a day. Make into 3 servings.

方名：李東垣清風散。
組成：
柴 胡 二錢，川 芎錢半，防風，薑活，酒炒黃 芩，姜半 夏，茯 苓，當 歸，丹皮，丹 參，各三錢，吳茱萸一錢，澤 瀉，川牛膝，赤芍，各四錢，懷牛膝八錢。
水煎服。每日一剤。分三次服。
