fumaric acid  延胡索酸

Translated and Edited By: Joe Hing Kwok Chu   

Fumaric acid esters are sometimes used to treat psoriasis, as it has been suggested that the condition is caused by an impairment of fumaric acid production in the skin.[1]

A starting dose is 60-105 mg daily, which may be gradually increased to as much as 1,290 mg per day. Side-effects include kidney or gastrointestinal disorders, as well as skin flushing; these are mainly caused by excess intake. Decreased white blood cell counts have been reported with prolonged use.

延胡索酸（反丁烯二酸，富馬酸）有時被用來治療銀痟病。有人認為皮膚上的延胡索酸生產降低而產生銀痟病。[1]

 起始劑量為每天60-105毫克，這可能是逐漸增加至高達1290毫克，每天。副作用包括腎臟或腸胃功能紊亂，以及皮膚潮紅;主要是由於過量攝入。有長時間使用延胡索酸有使到白血球數量降低的報告。
