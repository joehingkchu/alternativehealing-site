rou dou kou

 

by: Joe Hing Kwok Chu

 

 

Pharmaceutical name:

 Semen Myristicae Fragrantis

Biological name:

學名﹕

 Myristica cagayanensis Merr.

Pron. in Japanese:

 nikuzuku

Pron. in Korean:

yuktugu

Pron. in Cantonese:

yuk tao kao

Other Names:別名﹕

 rou kou, yu kou, rou guo

Common Name:

常用英文名﹕

 nutmegs

Distribution:

 Indonesia, Malaysia, Sri Langka, Taiwan, Guangdong, Yunnan, West Indies, and Brasil.

Properties characteristics:性味:

 acrid, warm, toxic

味辛，性溫, 有毒。

Channels meridians entered:

 歸經﹕

 spleen, stomach, large intestine

脾、胃、大腸

Actions & Indications:

主治﹕

Astringent to the intestine and relieve diarrhea; invigorate digestion. Often use in cold and deficient type of diarrhea, abdominal pain and flatulence, cold extremities, incontrollable diarrhea, lack of appetite and vomiting, weak digestion, vomiting and diarrhea due to parasites in children.

External use: sprains and bruises from injuries.

有澀腸止瀉、溫中行氣的功能。用於虛寒久瀉不止、脘腹冷痛、氣滯脹痛、脾胃虛寒、手足厥冷、滑泄不禁、食少嘔吐、消化不良、小兒乳積吐瀉等。

外用﹕跌打瘀傷﹐關節扭傷。

Medical function:

藥理作用﹕

 improves digestion and appetite.

有增進食慾、促進消化作用。

Samples of formulae:

處方舉例﹕

 rou dou kou wine extract: soak the outer wrapping of the seeds in rice wine of high alcohol content. Let sit in room temperature for about a month. Usage: for massaging bruises and sprain from injuries. (source: family formula of editor)

豆蔻花(衣)酒 (外用)﹕肉豆蔻種子的外衣﹐晒乾。浸入三蒸米酒中。放於室內大約一個月左右。用於扭傷﹐跌打傷。按摩患處。(編者祖傳藥方)

Chemical ingredients:

化學成份﹕

 Seeds contain:
  • evaporating oil of  5 ~ 15 %﹐
  • fat of  25 ~ 40 %﹐
  • starch.
  • Pinene
  • Sabinene
  • Camphene60~ 80%
  • Myristicin ~ 4%
  • Eugenol¡
  • Safrol
  • Elemicin
  • alpha-Thujene
  • Carene
  • Dipentene
  • Geraniol
  • Myristin40 ~73 %
  • Olein 3 %
  • Diarylpropanoid  I, IV, V, VIII, X

 

Wrappings of the seeds contain:

  • evaporting oil of 6 ~ 11 %
  • Macilenic acid ( C14H26O2)
  • Diarylpropanoid II, III, VI, IX, X
  • the rest same as the seed

Dosage:

用量:

 3 to 6 g

3～6克。

Cautions:

禁忌:

 not to use in cases of: 
  • diarrhea due to infection or food poisoning
  • during pregnancy.
  • hemorrhoids
  • gum infections

以下情形忌服

  • 濕熱瀉痢者或因食物細菌中毒
  • 懷孕婦人
  • 痔瘡
  • 牙周炎

Toxicity:

large dosage can cause death. Ingesting 7.6 g can cause dizziness and stupor.

大量服用肉豆蔻種子可導致瞳孔放大﹐昏迷﹐驚厥﹔並有致死亡的報導。服用7.6 克可致使頭昏失知的報導。

 

Editor note: dry seeds of rou dou kou are used in medicine or as cooking spice. In places that produce rou dou kou, the pickled fruit are being eaten as snack.

 

編者註﹕藥用或作香料用是用肉豆蔻的乾燥之種子﹐毒性大。一般在產地當水果用的肉豆蔻是用鹽或糖或加醋醃過的果肉﹐毒性小。
