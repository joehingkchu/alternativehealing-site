qing xin lian zi yin (Sooting lotus seed drink) 清心蓮子飲

Name of Formula:

qing xin lian zi yin (Comforting Lotus Seed drink)  (Source: he ji ju fang)

 

Application:

 spermatorrhea,  gonorrhea, diabetes, massive or incessant extramenstral vaginal bleeding with yinxu (yin deficient) and qixu (qi deficient) conditions:

 

Composition:

shi lian zi, ren shen, fu ling, huang qi, gan cao, mai dong, huang qin, di gu pi, che qian zi.

Boil with water and use as tea.

清心蓮子飲《和劑局方》

治 氣虛  與   陰虛，而 遺精，淋濁，消渴，以及婦人血崩﹐帶下等症。

组成：石蓮子﹐人 參﹐茯 苓，黃 耆﹐甘 草, 麥 冬  ﹐黃 芩﹐地骨皮，車 前 子 

水煎服。
