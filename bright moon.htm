Title of Song: 明月千里寄相思  The Bright Moon Sends my Love Messages for me to Thousands Miles Away

夜色茫茫罩四周，Everywhere the color of the night is blurry.
天邊新月如鉤， The new moon in the sky looks like a hook.
回憶往事恍如夢，Recalling the past, it is like a blurry dream,
重尋夢境何處求 ；Where do I find the dream again.

人隔千里路悠悠， We are thousands of miles apart, far far away.
未曾遙問心已愁， At a distance, before being able to inquire, my heart is already saddened.
請明月代問候， Oh moon, I plea that you help me send my respect.
思念的人兒淚常流；This longing person's tear is constantly flowing.
月色朦朦夜未盡，The moonlight is becoming indistinct, and the night is never ending

周遭寂寞寧静，The surrounding is lonely and quiet.
桌上寒燈光不明，Sitting by myself, feeling lonesome, the dim, cold lamp on the desk is
伴我獨坐苦孤零；accompanying me.
人隔千里無音訊，Separated by thousands of miles without any information,
欲待遙問終無憑，I want to ask but do not know where to begin.
請明月代傳信，I ask the moon to help me to send a message,

寄我片紙兒慰離情。 to send a piece of paper for me to comfort my sorrow from separating .
