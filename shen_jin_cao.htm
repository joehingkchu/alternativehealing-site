shen jin cao 伸筋草  （in English and Chinese 中英文)

 by Joe Hing Kwok Chu

Pharmaceutical name:

英文藥名﹕

Herba Lycopodii

Biological name:

植物學名﹕

Lycopodium  clavatum  L.

Lycopodium cernuum L. (垂穗石松(鋪地蜈蚣))

Lycopodium obscurum L. (玉 柏)

Pron. in Japanese:

日語發音﹕

shinkinsou

Pron. in Korean:

韓語發音﹕

Pron. in Cantonese:

粵音﹕

Common Name:

英文名﹕

common club moss, running Ground pine, running club moss, wolf's foot..

Distribution:

產地﹕

mainly produced in Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Hunan, Sichuan.

主產浙江，江蘇，湖南，四川。

除Lycopodium  clavatum  L. 外，浙江，江西，四川，廣西，廣东等省區尚用同属垂穗石松(鋪地蜈蚣) Lycopodium cernum L.的全草。

 characteristics)﹕

Medical functions:

藥理﹕

 

The ingredient α-onocerin of Lycopodium  clavatum has the effect of inhibiting acetylcholinesterase [2] and can be an herb for treating dementia cause by spleen deficient

Anti-inflammatory effect

hypotensive effect

Inhibiting Candida Albicans secreted aspartic proteases [note] [3].

伸筋草之化學成份, α-芒柄花萜醇有抑制乙 醯膽鹼酵素【註】。[3] 本品可能對因脾 虛所致健忘或痴呆症有效。

抗炎作用

降血壓作用

抑制白色念珠球菌分泌的羧基 蛋白酶 (天 冬氨酸蛋白酶)

解除小兒之痙攣性尿瀦留及便秘

Actions & Indications:

主治﹕

rheumatism, numbness in the skin, edema, physical injuries.

治風寒濕痺，關節酸痛，皮膚麻木，四肢軟弱，水腫， 跌打損傷。 
應用於風濕性關節炎及急性化膿性關節炎，或是頸，腰椎病，軟組織損傷，肌肉疼痛，筋脈拘緊等，可加 強通絡止痛之功效。

Dosage:

用 量﹕

6 ~ 15 g in decoction.

入煎劑﹕ 6 ~ 15 克。

Samples of formulae:

處方 舉例﹕

For treatment of sequela of polio: shen jin cao, nan she teng gen, song jie, xun gu feng 18 g each, wei ling xian 12 g, xi cao 7.5 g,

治小兒麻痺後遺症：伸筋草、南蛇藤根、松節、尋骨風各五錢，威靈仙三錢，茜草二錢，杜蘅五分。水煎服。(江西《中草藥學》)

Toxicity & Cautions:

毒素與禁忌﹕

not to use during pregnancies or during excessive bleeding.

Note: do not presoak this herb in water before baking.

孕婦及出血過多者忌服。

[注意] 本品炮製時 不宜水浸。

Note: Under normal condition, the aspartic proteases secreted by Candida Albicans does not increase the effect of causing diseases or harmful effect. {4]

註：天冬氨酸蛋白酶在正常條件下并不能增加白念菌的侵蝕性和致病力.[4]

【1】It is important to note that this herb is sometimes called kuan jin teng but not to confuse with Herba Tinosporae sinensis  which is mainly called kuan jin teng.

【2】 Planta Med. 2003 Mar;69(3):265-7.

【1】] 本品亦稱為寬筋藤，但不是學名為Tinospora sinensis 的 盒果藤 （亦稱 寬筋藤）

【3】乙醯膽鹼酵素 : 是一種促進水化(消滅)乙 醯膽鹼之酵素。乙醯膽鹼是 一種神經神經遞質。如果 神經神經遞質被消滅太 多，會導致臟躁， 憂鬱,  健忘或痴獃等症。
