da hua zi wei 大花紫薇

by: Joe Hing kwok Chu

A manuscript... writing and translation in progress

Pharmaceutical name:

英文藥名﹕

 

Botanical name:

學名﹕

Lagerstroemia speciosa (L.) Pers.

Pron. in Japanese:

日語發音﹕

 

Pron. in Korean:

韓語發音﹕

 

Pron. in Cantonese:

粵音﹕

 

Other Names:

別名﹕

 

Common Name:

英文名﹕

Queen-flower, Queen crape myrtle

Distribution:

產地﹕

原產於印度，中國及澳洲。現在主要分佈在印度，斯裡蘭卡，澳洲，馬來西亞，越南，菲律賓及中國的廣東，廣西，福建。

Properties

(characteristics)﹕

性味﹕

苦，微甘，溫

Channels (meridians) entered:

 

歸經﹕

Medical functions:

藥理﹕

 

具有降血糖的功能
減少肥胖機率
能夠降低膽固醇
具有鎮靜及抗菌的作用

Actions & Indications:

主治﹕

祛風涼血，消腫止痛，健胃利尿。用於風寒濕痺，糖尿病，脈管炎等。

Chemical ingredients:

化學成份﹕

克落糖酸(Corosolic acid), maslinic acid, 抑糖鞣酸(Ellagitannins), tannins,  衍生物包括 Lagerstroemin，Flosin-B，Reginin-A

Toxicity & Cautions:

毒素與禁忌﹕

may cause blood glucose excessively low when combined with drugs that can cause hypoglycemia, such as sulfonylureas (glyburide or glipizide, tolbutamide), or herbs that have hypoglycemic activity (Gymnema sylvestre 匙羹藤, American ginseng,

與降血糖的中藥同用可能會把血糖降得太低。. 降血糖的中藥有：人參，枸杞子，生地黃，丹參，玉米须，知母，赤芍，海带，當歸，葛根，黃連，山藥，玉竹，麥芽，茯苓，菟絲子，石膏，烏梅，蒼朮，白朮，玄參，仙鶴草，地骨皮，蒼耳子，澤瀉，桔梗，黃精，冬桑葉，仙靈脾等。
