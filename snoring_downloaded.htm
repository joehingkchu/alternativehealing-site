Snoring of Elderly 老年打鼾

Translated and Edited by: Joe Hing Kwok Chu    

Name of Formula: niu bang fang

Application: snoring in elderly

Composition:

niu bang zi、zhu ma gen、gan cao

Boil with water and use as tea.

Effectiveness:

  1. very effective: 89%

  2. effective: 15%

  3. not effective: 5%

Also see: San Zhong Kui Jian Tang

老年打鼾

處方﹕牛蒡子、苧 麻 根、甘 草.

顯效80％,有效15％,無效5％

出處﹕ 四川中醫 2002年 11期。

請參考﹕散腫潰堅湯
