Get 3 packages. Use one package per cooking.

(Use the cheaper kind of ginseng which is the Jilin ginseng.)

The recipe:

黃耆，熟地黃 各 24克，

昆布, 吉林人參，當 歸 各15克 ，

柴胡, 絲瓜絡 各9 克 ,

川 芎，枸 杞 ，通草，王不留行 各 6克 。

 

Make soup with 2 to 3 lbs of pig feet.

Simmer or double boiled for a few hours until

the pig feet pieces are very tender.

I used to cook them all day, 12 hours in double boiler.

Use one package every 3 to 4 days.
