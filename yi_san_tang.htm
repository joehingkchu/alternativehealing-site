yi huang san tang

by: Joe Hing kwok Chu

Uses: for vaginal discharge due to yeast or bacterial infection.  　

Prescription:

qian shi 20 g, shan yao 12 g, bai guo 12 g (smashed), huang bai 9 g, che qian zi 9 g. Add water and make into decoction. Use as tea.

Adjust the weight of herbs according to diagnosis.


易黃散湯

：芡實20 克﹐山藥12 克﹐白果12克打碎﹐黃粕9克﹐車前子9克。 水煎服。

藥份量﹐隨證加減。
