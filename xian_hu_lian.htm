xian hu lian宣胡連     (in English and Chinese 中英文)
 

by: Joe Hing kwok Chu

Pharmaceutical name:

英文藥名﹕

 Rhizoma Picrorhizae

Biological name:

拉丁文學名﹕

 Picrorhiza scrophulariaefolia Pennell (胡黃連)

Picrorhiza kurroa Royle ex Benth.(印度胡黃連)

Pron. in Japanese:

日語發音﹕

 

Pron. in Korean:

韓語發音﹕

 

Pron. in Cantonese:

粵音﹕

sun wu lian

Other Names:

別名﹕

 hu huang lian, hu lian

胡黃連，胡連

Common Name:

英文名﹕

 

Distribution:

產地﹕

 

Properties

(characteristics)﹕

性味﹕

bitter, cold

味苦，性寒。

Medical functions:

藥理﹕

 Medical functions:
  1. anti pyretic,
  2. anti-inflammation,
  3. improves digestion,
  4. rids of parasites
  5. antibiotic,
  6. mild laxative

 

藥理﹕

  1. 解熱，
  2. 消炎，
  3. 健胃，
  4. 驅蟲
  5. 抗菌，
  6. 輕瀉

Channels (meridians) entered:

歸經﹕

heart, large intestine, liver, and stomach channels. 

心﹐大腸﹐ 肝﹐胃。

Actions & Indications:

主治﹕

parasites in children, influenza, yinxu (yin deficient) with fever, liver and gall bladder diseases with fever, TB of the lungs with fever, diarrhea due to infection, inflammation of the skin due to fungal infection, softening of the cornea.

小兒疳積，流感病毒，陰虛發熱，肝膽病發熱，肺結核發熱，發熱下痢，真菌性皮膚炎，角膜軟化症。

Chemical ingredients:化學成份﹕

to be loaded

Dosage:

用量﹕

10 to 20 g per day. Concentrated extract 0.5 to 2 g. Adjust according to syndrome and body condition.

10～20克；濃縮中藥：0.5～2.0克。可依病症與體質酌量增減使用量。
