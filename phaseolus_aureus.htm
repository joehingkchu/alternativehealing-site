mung bean 綠荳

 

by: Joe Hing Kwok Chu

 

Translation and writing in progress........

 

Pharmaceutical name:

英文藥名﹕

  

Biological name:

拉丁文學名﹕

  1. Vigna radiata(L.)R.Wilczek.

  2. Phaseolus aureus Roxb.   

Pron. in Japanese:

日語發音﹕

  

Pron. in Korean:

韓語發音﹕

Pron. in Cantonese:

粵音﹕

 luk tao

Other Names:

別名﹕

  

Common Name:

外文名﹕

  

Mung bean, Black dhal, Black gram, Black mung, Golden gram, Gram bean, Green gram, Red mung bean, Urd

Distribution:

產地﹕

Main production areas: India, Thailand, Afganistan, Phillipines, China, South East Asia and America.

 

世界上綠豆主產國有印度、泰國、阿富汗、菲律賓 、美國等國。
在中國黃河、淮河流域以及華北和東北平原各省都有綠豆栽培

 

Properties

(characteristics)﹕

性味﹕

sweet, cold, non toxic

 

甘寒無毒
Medical functions:  

Channels (meridians) entered:

歸經﹕

 heart, spleen

入心﹐胃經

Actions & Indications:

主治﹕

rids of edema, possesses antipyretic effect, diuretic effects, rids of headache, calming, antidote for toxicity of arsenic, and poisons from plants or minerals.

 

 

消腫下氣、壓熱解毒、治丹毒、煩熱風疹、利尿止渴、厚腸胃。作枕明目、治頭風、頭痛、補益元氣、和調五臟、安精神、行十二經脈、潤皮膚、解一切草木金石砒霜毒

 

Chemical ingredients:化學成份﹕

 See appendix below

Dosage:

用量﹕

 30 ~ 60 g

Samples of formulae

:處方舉例﹕

 mung bean soup: lu do 60 g, chen pi 3 g. Simmer for about one hour with water to make soup.

Modern Research:

現代研究﹕

  

Cautions:

禁忌﹕

 Dot not use in cases of weak digestion.

脾胃虛寒者忌食。
