hepatitis B decoction

 

Edited by: by: Joe Hing Kwok Chu

 

Composition:

dan shen 20 g, gan cao 15 g, hu zhang 15 g, yu jin  10 g,

wu wei zi 10 g, nu zhen zi 15 g, bai zhu 10 g .

 

Add water and simmer for 45 minutes to make into decoction.

Make into 3 servings per day.  12 weeks is a therapy period.

It can be repeated if needed.

 

See formula to lower AST and ALT

See herbs with anti hepatitis B virus properties.

乙型肝炎藥方

 

組成：

丹参 20 g,甘草 15 g, 虎杖 15 g, 鬱金 10 g, 五味子 10 g, 女貞子15 g,  白朮 10 g .

水煎。作一日量。分三次服。12週為一療程。如有須要，可以加多一療程。

 

看降低AST 與 ALT 之配方。

 

看能乙型肝炎病毒之中藥
