Chinese Herb Formula for Yeast Infection

Compiled and Edited By: Joe Hing Kwok Chu

Vagina yeast infection herb formula 2

Diagnostics: dampness is high and fire is weak. Liver is stagnant and spleen and stomach qi are weak.

Formula: Wan Dai Tang

tu chao bai zhu 38 g, chao shan yao 38 g,  ren shen 7.5 g,  chao bai shao 19 g,

che qian zi 11 g,  zhi cang zhu 11 g,  gan cao 4 g, chen pi 2 g, he jing jie 2 g,

chai hu 2 g

Boil with water. Take one prescription a day; make into two servings. Add water and re-boil the herbs next day.  Take 6 prescriptions in 12 days.

 

If the yeast infection is not clear by then, use the formula gu zhen tang

Formula: gu zhen tang

ren shen 7.5 g,  ji pi ( including the white portion) 2 g,  gan jiang mo 7.5 g,

bai gui hua 16 pieces,  chai hu 4 g, zhi gan cao 4 g,  yu li ren (ground) 4 g,

sheng huang qin 4 g ( separated from the rest of herbs)

Boil all herbs, except sheng huang qin, with 1000 cc of water until 600 cc. remains.  Add sheng huang qin and boil until 350 cc. Drink while warm, before meal. After a few minutes, eat breakfast.
