chrysin 白楊素　　     (in English and Chinese 中英文)
 

by: Joe Hing kwok Chu

 

Chrysin
Synonyms: 5,7 - hydrogen flavonoids, 5,7 - dihydroxyflavone.
Molecular formula: C15H10O4
Molecular Weight: 254.24
Pale yellow prismatic crystals. The melting point of 285 ° C, soluble in alkali hydroxide solution at room temperature, dissolved in acetone, slightly soluble in ether, ethanol, and chloroform, insoluble in water.

Medical function: anti-cancer, lowering blood pressure, anti-heart brain blood vessel diseases, anti-bacterial, anti-inflammatory

Storage method: store in cool, dark places. Avoid light and high temperature.

白楊素
別名：5,7-氫黃酮，5,7-二羥基黃酮，柯菌
分 子 式：C15H10O4
分 子 量：254.24

淡黃色棱柱形結晶。熔點285℃，溶於氫氧化鹼溶液，室溫下溶解丙酮，微溶於乙醚，乙醇和氯仿，不溶於水。
 

藥理作用：白楊素能抗癌，降血脂，防心腦血管疾病，抗菌，消炎等
　　
　　
保存方法：陰涼乾燥，避光，避高溫
