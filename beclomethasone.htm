Beclomethasone

medical function and uses:

It is a strong external application glucocorticoid drug for anti inflammation, anti allergy, and anti itching. It can inhibit mucus excretion of the bronchi, get rid of inflammation of the mucus of the bronchi, and of spasm of the bronchi. The function of constricting the blood vessels of the skin is five times that of hydrocortisone and can easily absorbed by the skin. It takes effect in thirty minutes after application.
