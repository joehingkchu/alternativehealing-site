Shen Xu (Kidney Deficient) 腎虛

by: Joe Hing Kwok Chu    按此看中文

A term used in Traditional Chinese medicine.

Symptoms include weakness and soreness of lower back, weakness in knees and legs, pain in heels, losing hair, bad teeth, tinnitus, spermatorrhea, impotence, irregular menses, dizziness, blurred vision and premature aging.

When yangxu condition also exits, it is called shenyangxu.

When yinxu condition also exits, it is called shenyinxu.

Shen xu condition can exist without yangxu or yingxu.

腎虛症狀﹕

腰背疼痛﹐脛痠膝軟腳跟痛﹐髮脫﹐齒搖﹐耳鳴﹐耳聾﹐ 遺精﹐陽萎﹐月經不調﹐眼花﹐頭暈﹐早衰。

腎虛兼 陽虛 稱為腎陽虛。

腎虛兼陰虛稱為腎陰虛。

腎虛可單獨存在﹐不一定有陽虛或陰虛症狀。
