là gēn 辣根   ( in English and Chinese 中英文)

by: Joe Hing kwok Chu    朱興國編 譯   

Pharmaceutical name:

英文藥名﹕

 Radix Armoraciae rusticanae

Botanical name:

拉丁生物學名﹕

 Armoracia rusticana（(Lam.) Gaertn.，B.Mey.et scherb

Pron. in Japanese:

日語發音﹕

 seiyouwashabi

Pron. in Korean:

韓語發音﹕

 seoyang gochu naeng-i

Pron. in Cantonese::

粵音﹕

 laat6 gan1

Other Names:

別名﹕

 ma luo bo (literally: horse radish) 馬蘿蔔

Common Name:

英文名﹕

 horse radish

Distribution:

產地﹕

 Originally produced in eastern Europe and Turkey. Now it is grown in Europe, South Australia and in California and Wisconsin, Illinois. Illinois is the largest producing area in the world Later it spread to China. Early cultivation in Qingdao, Shanghai suburb of China, and also other suburban or vegetables processing bases there are small-scale cultivation.

原產於東歐和土耳。現在產於歐洲，澳大利亞州南部，美國的加利福尼亞州和威斯康星州，伊利諾伊州。伊利諾伊州是世界上最大的產區。後來蔓延到中國。早期在中國的青島，上海郊區種植，其他郊區或蔬菜加工基地有小規模種植。

Properties

characteristics﹕

性味﹕

 acrid, warm.

辛，溫。

Channels meridians entered:

 

歸經﹕

 stomach, gall bladder, bladder.

胃，膽，膀胱。
Medical functions:

藥理﹕

 
  1. antibiotic
  2. anti-inflammatory
  3. anti-cancer  [2]
  4. de-worming

 

  1. 抗菌
  2. 抗炎
  3. 抗腫瘤 [2]
  4. 驅蟲

Actions & Indications:

主治﹕

 Sciatica, arthritis, difficulty in urination, weak digestion, cholecystitis, and hernia pain, as anthelmintics, diuretic

坐骨神經痛，關節炎，排尿困難，消化不良，膽囊炎，疝痛，作驅腸蟲藥，利尿剤。

Toxicity & Cautions:

毒素與禁忌﹕

 Because it is acrid and hot in taste, do not overdose. Eating too much can cause diarrhea and sweating.

因為其性味辛辣，內服不宜過量。如服用能引起腹瀉和出汗。
