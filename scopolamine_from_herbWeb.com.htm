Common Sources of Scopolamine (Hoscine)

compiled by Joe Hing Kwok Chu

Scopolamine is a narcotic that can cause pharmaceutical high.

It can be extracted from the these plants:

Jimson weed is weed which grows wild across much of the United States.

 

Atropa belladonna - BELLADONNA, BLACK CHERRY, DEADLY NIGHTSHADE, DWALE, POISON BLACK CHERRY

Brugmansia arborea - BORRACHERO, FLORIPONDIO, HUACACACHU, HUANTO, MAICOA, TOA, TONGA

Datura candida - TREE DATURA

Datura inoxia - ANGEL'S-TRUMPET, JIMSON WEED, PRICKLYBURR, THORNAPPLE

Datura stramonium - JIMSON WEED, JIMSONWEED, STRAMONIUM, THORNAPPLE

Mandragora officinalis - MANDRAGORA, MANDRAKE, SATAN'S APPLE

Solandra brevicalyx - HUEIPATL, KIELI, TECOMAXOCHITL

 

Dubosia, is a native plant of the Kingaroy district of Queensland, Australia.
