tian ma

By: Joe Hing Kwok Chu

Pharmaceutical name:
Biological name: Gastrodia elata Bl., Orchidaceaea
Pron. in Japanese:
Pron. in Korean:
Pron. in Cantonese:
Common Name: Gastrodia Tuber
Distribution: It is mainly produced in Sichuan, Yunnan and Guizhou provinces.  Other producing regions include Northeast China and North China.
Properties (characteristics) Sweet in taste and neutral in nature.
Channels (meridians) entered: Liver.
Actions & Indications: 1. Calming the endopathic wind and relieving convulsion and spasm. For symptoms of liver-wind, no matter if cold type or heat type.2. Calming the liver and suppressing liver-yang. For such symptoms as vertigo and headache.3. Removing obstruction in the channels to relieve pain. For arthralgia due to the wind, cold and dampness, or for numbness and paralysis caused by liver and kidney deficiency.
Medical Function: 1.Effect on the CNS: sedation,  anti-seizure, painkilling effect. 2. Effect on heart and blood vessels: protect the heart muscle lacking circulation, improve blood flow in the coronary artery, improve blood flow to the brain, mitigate the effect of adrenalin on blood vessel constriction, lower blood pressure.3. Effect on tolerance of lack of oxygen. 4. Improve the immune system. 5. Other effects: increase the skin temperature; increase the expansion and contraction ability of the smooth muscle of the intestine; improve the secretion of the bile in the gall bladder (vanillyl alcohol); slow down breathing; inhibits 5-HT, PGE2; 6. Vanilline can be absorbed in the small intestine rapidly and shows up in the liver and the brain, especially the cerebrum, and the medication quickly permeates the blood-brain barrier. Vanilline is excreted through the urine within 10-12 hours. 7. Toxicity: Relatively safe.
Chemical ingredients: vanilline, vanillyl alcohol, gastrodin, p-hydroxymethylphenyl-beta-D-glucopyranoside, p-hydroxybenzyl alcohol, Beta-sitosterol, daucosterol, citric acid, citric acid mono-methyl ester, palmitic acid, sucrose, p-hydroxybenzaldehyde, succinic acid, bis (4-hydroxybenzyl) ether mono-Beta-D-glucopyranoside, 4-hydroxybenzyl methyl ether, 4-(4'-hydroxybenzyloxy) benzyl methyl ether, bis (4-hydroxybenzyl) ether, 4-(Beta-D-glucopyranosyloxy) benzyl alcohol, tris [4-(Beta-D-glucopyranosyloxy) benzyl] citrate, Parishin. 3, 4-dihydroxybenzyl aldehyde, 4, 4'-dihydroxydiphenyl methane, p-hydroxybenzyl ethyl ether, 4, 4'-dihydroxydibenzyl ether, 4-ethoxy methyl phenyl-4'-hydroxy benzyl ether. A variety of trace minerals: Iron, Fluorine, Manganese, Zinc, Iodine. Also contains anti-fungus protein.
Dosage: As a tea, 3 -9 g daily. As powder, 1 - 1.5 g each time.
Samples of formulae:
Modern Usage: For epilepsy,for external injury of the brain, as a painkiller, for neurasthenia, for depression, for the blood-vessel type of migraine. No undesirable side effects. It does not create a tolerance.
Cautions: Should not be used in case of yin deficiency.
