Medical functions:

藥理﹕

 

 ya ma ren (flax seed) and its root contain large amount of lignin which is a strong anti-oxidant and can inhibit the production harmful carcinogenic substances.

anti-cancer of the intestine effect: ya ma ren (flax seed) contains large amount of soluble fibre which can reduce the toxicity of the digested substances and speed up the secretion of the feces thus reduces the duration of the time of stimulating substance on the mucous membrane of the instestine.

ya ma root can help the function of the kidneys to excrete toxin.

ya ma root contains large amount of omega -3 fatty acid which can lower blood lipids and lower the LDL in the blood and can reduce arteriosclerosis.

inhibits the growth of the prostate cells: in vitro studies showed that the lignin of the ya ma ren (flax seed) can inhibit the growth of the cells of the prostate gland itself.

亚麻和其根部含大量的木质素 (lignin) 。具有很强的抗氧化特性，能有效遏制促使癌生成的有害化学物质。

对防治肠癌的作用﹕富含纤维素，可减轻肠道中消化物產生的不良影响﹐加快肠道中废物的排泄，减短对肠内黏膜有剌激性的物质在肠道中停留的时间。

亚麻根有助于肾脏的排毒功能 。

亚麻根富含的脂肪酸，可降低血脂，低血液中的低密度胆固醇 (LDL)﹐治疗血管硬化。

控制攝護腺細胞生長。 體外的實驗證明 亞麻仁木質素 (lignin)可以控制攝護腺細胞本身之生長。

Actions & Uses:

主治﹕

 improves bowel movement

lower blood lipids

Seed must be ground for consumption because of the tough seed coat that is indigestible and should be taken with water since the mucilage absorbs 5 times the weight of the seed.

It is not advisable to use flax seed in bake food since the fatty acid omega-3 cannot withstand high heat and can become carcinogenic.

Flax Seeds are the highest dietary source of  lignin. They contain 75 times the quantity of  Lignin compared to the other plants that contain next highest source of Plant Lignin. The majority of the Plant lignin component of flax seeds is located in the outer husk of the Flax Seed .

Cautions:

禁忌﹕

 Omega-3 is a highly unstable fat and can become rancid quickly and become carcinogenic. Do not use flax seed oil for deep fat frying. Store flax seed and flax oil in black container and refrigerate. Discard seed or oil that smell fishy or rancid which are highly toxic.

Seed should be washed before using to avoid the mold , Aspergillus niger,  which is toxic for the respiration system and the mold , Aspergillus flavus,  which is toxic for the liver.  These mold are commonly found in grains and are extremely toxic.

　

Linamarin, after hydrolysis can release free hydrogen cyanide acid, which can suffocate and poison the cells.

亚麻苦甙（linamarin），经水解後可析出游離态的氫氰酸，致组织细胞窒息中毒。

 

A diet rich in flax seed seems to reduce the size, aggressiveness and severity of tumors in mice that have been genetically engineered to develop prostate cancer, according to new research from Duke University Medical Center. And in 3 percent of the mice the flax seed diet kept them from getting the disease at all. (From news release issued by Duke University Medical Center).
