Translated and edited by: Joe Hing Kwok Chu

 

Age spots

Age spots were called liver spots. They are flat, brown or black spots that can appear on areas of the skin exposed to the sun. They have nothing to do with the liver or liver function. [1]

 

The cause: exposure to ultra violet light, like sunlight or tanning lights.

The inducing causes and therapy methods are as follows.

 

Inducing causes and therapy methods:

 

For qi and blood stasis:

Use:

  • huang qi gui zhi wu wu tang or

  • ba zhen tang, add yan hu suo, hong hua, tao ren.

 

For heat in blood:

  • Use: wen qing yin, or

  • yu nu jian, add chi shao, sheng di huang, tao ren, dan pi.

 

For pixu (spleen deficient):

Use:

  • gui pi tang add chi shao, tao ren, bai zhi, ge gen.

 

For brown spot with menopausal syndrome:

Use:

  • dan zhi xiao yao san with si wu tang add zi che he; or

  • dang gui shao yao tang or

  • gui zhi fu ling wan  add yi yi ren。
