da bai qu cai 大白屈菜

by: Joe Hing kwok Chu


　　　

Pharmaceutical name:

英文藥名﹕

 Herba Chelidonii

Botanical name:

學名﹕

 Chelidonium majus L.

Pron. in Japanese:

日語發音﹕

  

Pron. in Korean:

韓語發音﹕

  

Pron. in Cantonese:

粵音﹕

 daai6 baak6 gwat6 coi3

Other Names:

別名﹕

 shan huang lian 山黃連，tu huang lian 土黃連，niu jin hua 牛金花，ba bu jing八步緊，duan chang cao (in Beijing) 斷腸草 [北京]

Common Name:

英文名﹕

 Greater Calandine Herb

Distribution:

產地﹕

 Shandong, Henan, Shaanxi, Gansu, Jiangsu, Jiangxi, provinces, northeastern and northern part of China.; also found in Korea, Japan, Middle East and Europe.

分佈於我國東北，華北及山東，河南，陝西，甘肅，江蘇，江西等省。朝鮮，日本，中亞地區至歐洲也有分布。 

Properties

(characteristics)﹕

性味﹕

 bitter, cool, toxic.

苦，涼。有毒。

Channels (meridians) entered:

歸經﹕
Medical functions:

藥理﹕

 

  

Actions & Indications:

主治﹕

 arresting  pain, arresting cough, soothing asthma, resolution. Being used in stomach pain, chronic bronchitis, whopping cough.

鎮痛，止咳，平喘，消腫。用於胃痛，慢性支氣管炎，百日咳。

Chemical ingredients:

化學成份﹕

 白屈菜鹼（chelidonine），白屈菜紅鹼（chelerythrine） ，血根鹼（sanguinarine）α-，β-，γ-高白屈菜鹼（α-，β-，γ-homochelidonine） ，普托品（protopine），小檗鹼 (berberine)，黃連鹼 (Coptisine），白屈菜酸 （chelidonic acid），膽鹼 (Choline），芸香甙 （rutoside）等 etc。

Dosage:

用量﹕

 take decoction orally: 3 to 9 g. External application: use appropriate amount. Grind and make into past and apply to affected areas.

內服：煎湯，3 ~  9 克。外用適量，研粉調膏或搗爛敷患處。
