Differentiating jiang huang, yu jin, shu gu jiang huang  and e zhu

姜黄，鬱金，束骨姜黄，莪朮之分別           按此看中文。Click here for Chinese

By: Joe Hing Kwok Chu

 

 

Differentiating the varieties and the confusion::

  • Curcuma longa L﹐ (jiang huang 薑黄); also called Curcuma domestica Val.

  • Curcuma kwangsinenssis S.G. Leee et C.F. Ling L. (Guangxi e zhu 廣西莪朮，桂鬱金 )

  • Curcuma aromatica Salisb. (yu jin 鬱金 )

  • Curcuma zedoaria (berg) Rosc. (e zhu 莪朮 )

  • Curcuma wenyujin Y. H. Chen et C. Ling (温鬱金)

  • Curcuma phaeocaulis Val. (蓬莪朮, 綠絲鬱金)

  • Curcuma xanthorrhiza  Roxb. (束骨姜黄)

He yu jin (black yu jin) is also called: wen yu jin, wen yi jin, chuan yu jin is produced in the district of Wen Zhou of Zhejiang province. Chuan in this case does not mean Sichuan province but it means river. Wen does not mean warm or mild. It means the district Wen Zhou.

Guang yu jin does not mean it is produced in Guangdong. Guang (廣) in this case means "broad“. It is mainly produced in Sichuan. It is the root of Curcuma longa  It is also called "huang si yu" (huang si means yellow silk) and "jiang huang".

E zhu (Curcuma zedoaria) dry herb is usually in thicker slices and grey in color.

Yu jin dry herb is usually thinner and in smaller slices than e zhu and translucent and much paler than jiang huang (turmeric).

Shu gu jiang huang (Curcuma xanthorrhiza  Roxb.) is lighter in color compared to jiang huang (turmeric) and the roots are more than 10 times larger than jiang huang. It is also called da jiang huang, meaning large jiang huang.

藥材商品因產地和品種不同， 名稱類似而植物不相同。有名稱相同而植物不同，因而産生紊亂情形：

  • 薑黄 Curcuma longa L﹐ 學名亦稱為 Curcuma domestica Val.

  • 廣西莪朮，桂鬱金 Curcuma kwangsinenssis S.G. Leee et C.F. Ling L.

  • 鬱金Curcuma aromatica Salisb.

  • 莪朮 Curcuma zedoaria (berg) Rosc.

  • 束骨姜黄 Curcuma xanthorrhiza  Roxb.

温鬱金是 Curcuma wenyujin Y. H. Chen et C.Ling 之根莖‘ 稱為温鬱金﹐ 黑鬱金﹐川鬱金，川益金。為產于浙江温州的植物 鬱金的乾燥塊根。『温』不是指温和；而是指温州。『川』不是指四川。可能是指甌江。

廣鬱金(Curcuma longa L)主產於四川，為薑黃的塊根，色鮮黃。又 稱為 『黄絲鬱金』 或『薑黃』。

川鬱金 (Curcuma wenyujin Y. H. Chen et C. Ling)主產於浙江溫州，又名溫鬱金，為鬱金的塊根，色暗灰。

蓬莪朮，別名綠絲鬱金 (Curcuma phaeocaulis Val. )

薑黄與鬱金功效相似而略異，川鬱金(鬱金)偏重於行氣解鬱，廣鬱金 (薑黄)偏重於活血化瘀。

束骨薑黄，別名大薑黄但不是薑黄。一般用於治肝炎與産婦用來催乳。

 

四種飲片外觀不同：

  1. 莪朮飲片通常較大與厚而不透明，灰褐色。

  2. 鬱金飲片較小，色浅而半透明，不香。

  3. 薑黄飲片色黄，味香。

  4. 束骨薑黄飲片較莪朮大而黄，但較薑黄淡。
