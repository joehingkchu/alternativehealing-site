gui jiu　鬼臼

by: Joe Hing Kwok Chu

Links to picture of plants

Picture of plant,  another picture of plant in Chinese site,

 

Pharmaceutical name:Rhizoma Podophyllum
Biological name:Podophyllum emodi Wall, var. chinensis Sprague
Other Names:別名﹕tao er qi, xiao ye lian, xi zhang gui jiu, shu tai, tong kuai zi, e mu sai

桃兒柒﹐桃兒七，桃耳七﹐鬼臼, 小葉蓮, 西藏鬼臼,素苔﹐銅筷子﹐鵝木塞

Pron. in Cantonese:kua kao
Common Name:mayapple, mandrake, Indian apple, wild lemon, duck's foot
Distribution:Shanxi, Gansu, Qinghai, Sichuan, Yunnan,  Xizang

陜西﹑甘肅﹑青海﹑四川﹑雲南 和 西藏

Properties (characteristics)Sweet, neutral and toxic

甘﹑平﹑有毒.

Channels (meridians) entered: 
Actions & Indications:For rheumatoid arthritis, pain, injuries caused by impacts, cancer

袪風濕, 止痛. 用於風濕疼痛, 跌打損傷. 

Medical function:(1) Stimulate digestion function

(2) Anti bacteria

(1)能刺激胃壁神經，使消化機能亢進
(2)抗菌。

Samples of formulae: 
Chemical ingredients:根及根茎含鬼臼毒素(podophyllotoxin)、去氧鬼臼毒素deoxy-podophyllotoxin)、異苦鬼臼酮((isopicropodophyllone)、金絲桃甙(hyperin)等

 

Dosage:

劑量

used in concoction: 3 to 10 g. For pills and powder forms: 1.3 to 3 g.

內服入煎劑，常用量3～10克。 入丸散1.5~3g, 

Toxicity:

毒 性 :

Discomfort in the upper abdomen, mild diarrhea, pain in the lower abdomen, headache, dizziness. Large amount causes coma, shock, paralysis in the limbs and serious injury in the brain. 

主要有上腹不適﹑輕度腹瀉﹑下腹疼痛﹑頭痛頭暈‧大劑量會引起昏迷﹑休克﹑四肢癱瘓和腦部嚴重受損 . 
 輕 者 停 藥 或 減 量 , 症 狀 即 可 消 失 . 大 劑 量 者 則 洗 胃及 搶 救 . 

Treatment of Toxicity:

解 救 方 法 :

 

In mild cases, stop using the herb or decrease the dosage then symptoms will subside. In case of large dosage, promote vomiting.

輕 者 停 藥 或 減 量 , 症 狀 即 可 消 失 . 大 劑 量 者 則催吐或 洗 胃 .
