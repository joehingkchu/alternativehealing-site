Learn simple effective techniques that have been perfected in China for centuries. Stretches, self-massage,

acupressure, postures and conscious breathing patterns are easy and fun to learn, and they work! The "Eight Pieces

of Brocade" and other qigong programs can help people of ALL ages look and feel better  longer.

 

Joe Hing Kwok Chu, the instructor, is a master of qigong. He has been teaching in the U.S. since 1967, and for many

years before that in his native Asia. He studied qigong, kung fu, and tai chi chuan with a broad range of famous

teachers. He began qigong study in 1951 at Pao's Spiritual Institution in Hong Kong, where he was distinguished in

different schools of qigong. He learned injury medicine as an apprentice before studying the full breadth of Chinese

medicine in medical school in Hong Kong, where he graduated with honors.

Master Chu also offers private classes in qigong, basing the program of instruction on each person's particular

condition, at his Academy of Shaolin System.
