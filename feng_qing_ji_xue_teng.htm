feng qing ji xue teng 鳳慶雞血藤

by: Joe Hing kwok Chu    朱興國編 譯     (in English and Chinese 中英文)

Pharmaceutical name:

Radix et Caulis kadsurae

Botanical name:

Kadsura interior A.C.Smith  (内南五味子)

Kadsura heteroclita (Roxb.) Craib．(異形南五味子)

產地﹕

Daly Dosage:

每日用量﹕

For decoction for oral consumption: 15 to 30 g, or can be rendered into paste. Take with water or rice wine. Twice a day.
