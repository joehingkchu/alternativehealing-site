sang huang 桑黃  (in English and Chinese 中英文)

Translated by: Joe Hing kwok Chu      朱興國編譯

Pharmaceutical name:

英文藥名﹕

Fructificatio Phellini lintei

Botanical name:

學名﹕

Phellinus linteus

Phellinus igniarius (L. ex Fr) Quel.

Phellinus baumii

Pron. in Japanese:

日語發音﹕

meshimakobu

Pron. in Korean:

韓語發音﹕

sanghwang

Pron. in Cantonese::

粵音﹕

 

Other Names:

別名﹕

huo mu ceng gong jun

火木層孔菌.

Common Name:

英文名﹕

Black Hoof Fungus, fleckedflesh polypore,

Distribution:

產地﹕

Phellinus linteus is distributed in central America. P. baumii is distributed in Eastern Asia.

Phellinus linteus 分佈於中美洲，Phellinus baumii  分佈於 東亞地區；在中國分佈於東北、華北、西北及四川、雲南等地。

Properties

(characteristics)﹕

性味﹕

slightly bitter, cold.

微苦，寒。

Channels (meridians) entered:

 

歸經﹕

Medical functions:

藥理﹕

 

 

Actions & Indications:

主治﹕

For treatment of urinary stone, urinary bleeding, vagina discharge, heavy vaginal bleeding not during menses, benign type of female tumors, fluid retention in the hypochondrium, pixu (spleen deficient) with diarrhea.

主治淋病，崩漏帶下，症瘕積聚，癖飲， 脾虛泄瀉。

Chemical ingredients:

化學成份﹕

 

Dosage:

用量﹕

For oral consumption: boil with water, 15-30 g. Make into one serving. Twice a day.

0.5～1兩，水煎1次服完，日服2次。

Samples of formulae:

處方舉例﹕

 

治脫肛瀉血不止： 香附一兩(焙)，桑黃一兩(微炙)。上藥，掇羅為末，煉蜜和丸，如梧桐子大。每於食前，以粥飲下二十丸。(聖惠方)

治婦人勞損，月水不斷，血竭暫止，小勞輒劇： 桑黃搗羅為末，每於食前，以熱酒調下二錢。(聖惠方)

治瘰癧潰爛： 桑黃菇五錢，水紅豆一兩，百草霜三錢，青苔二錢，冰片一分。為末，雞子白調敷；以車前、艾葉、桑皮煎湯洗之。(纂要畜方)

Modern Research:

現代研究﹕

 

Toxicity & Cautions:

毒素與禁忌﹕

sang huang and its extract is seem to be not harmful to human body. No report of side effect has been found on large and long term usage.

桑黃及提取物被認為對人體無毒無害。長期大劑量服用尚沒有見到任何毒副作用的報告。

《全國中草藥彙編》
