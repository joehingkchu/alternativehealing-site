Purslane Lamb (or Goat) Stew 

馬齒莧羊肉湯

Servings: 6 to 8

1½ cups

dried black-eyed peas (眉豆), soaked overnight and drained

 

 

1½ cups

dried chickpeas (雞豆Garbanzo Beans), soaked overnight and drained
   

1½ cups

mini brown lentils(小扁豆), picked over and rinsed

1 bunch

Chinese parsley 芫荽 (香菜)

1/4 cup

olive oil (olive oil can not stand heat in stir frying. It is better to use other vegetable oil, or  concentrated coconut milk.)

 

 

1 lb

boneless leg of lamb or goat, (or shank) cut into 1/2-inch dice

 

 

1

medium onion(洋蔥), finely chopped

5 slices

fresh ginger 生薑

6 teaspoons

 tomato paste 番茄糊

3 pounds

purslane (馬齒莧),  (do not discard thick stems), cut into bite sizes

 

 

 

1 1/2 cups

coarse bulgur (cracked wheat)

 

 

2 tablespoons

 minced garlic 大蒜

 

 

3 tablespoons

 fresh lemon juice or use sliced asam 亞森片

 

 

some

Salt or thin soy sauce

 

 

1 tablespoon

 dried spearmint, leaves crushed to a fine powder 薄荷葉

 

 
   

 

 

to taste

Freshly ground black pepper 黑胡椒

 

 

 

Trimmed scallions and lemon wedges, for serving 蔥﹐檸檬塊

 

 

1. Rinse the black-eyed peas and chickpeas. Pour them into separate medium saucepans and cover with several inches of water. Cover and cook over moderate heat until tender, about 20 minutes for the black-eyed peas and 1 hour for the chickpeas. Save all the  liquid. For cooking. (The trace minerals in the bean provide the taste.)

2. Meanwhile, in a medium saucepan, combine the lentils with 12 cups of water, cover partially and cook over moderate heat until tender, about 40 minutes. Save all the  liquid. For cooking. (The trace minerals in the bean provide the taste.)

3. In a large, enameled cast-iron casserole, heat 2 tablespoons of the oil and brown the some of the smashed garlic Add lamb and cook over moderate heat, stirring occasionally, until browned, about 5 minutes. Stir in the onion, cover and cook until softened but not browned, about 2 minutes. Add the tomato paste, and 1/2 cup of water and bring to a simmer. Cover and cook, stirring once or twice, until the mixture begins to caramelize, about 20 minutes.

4. Add the purslane, bulgur (cracked wheat) and the reserved chickpea and lentil cooking liquids to the casserole. Cover and cook for 10 minutes. Add the chickpeas, black-eyed peas, lentils, garlic and enough water to barely cover. Cover and simmer for 5 minutes. Remove from the heat, stir in the lemon juice and season with salt.

5. In a small skillet, heat the remaining 2 tablespoons of oil. Add the spearmint, and 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper. When the oil begins to sizzle, give it a stir and drizzle it over the stew. Stir once and let stand for 30 minutes. Serve the stew at room temperature or let cool, then refrigerate and serve chilled the following day. Pass the scallions and lemon at the table.
