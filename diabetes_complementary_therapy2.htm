Diabetes Complementary of Chinese and Western Therapy

The complementary therapy for diabetes of Traditional Chinese herb medicine, acupuncture, tui na, qigong and Western medicine has been a great success in China.

Majority of diabetes mellitus sufferers, when using Western medicine alone, eventually die of cardio-vascular complications. According to some experience of some major modern hospital reports in China, correct applications of Traditional Chinese medicine together with modern drugs can prevent those complications.

The traditional Chinese medicine (internal herbal medicine) diagnostics are based on zheng, which is a system of clinical diagnostics that emphasize the overall functions of the human body.
