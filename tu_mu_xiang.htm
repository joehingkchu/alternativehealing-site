tu mu xiang 土木香

by: Joe Hing kwok Chu    朱興國編 譯     in English and Chinese 中英文

Pharmaceutical name:

英文藥名﹕

Radix Inulae

Botanical name:

拉丁生物學名﹕

Inula helenium L.

Inula racemosa L.  (藏木香)

Pron. in Japanese:

日語發音﹕

dobokku

Pron. in Korean:

韓語發音﹕

t'omokhyang

Pron. in Cantonese::

粵音﹕

tou2 muk6 hoeng1

Other Names:

別名﹕

qi mu xiang 祁木香，ma nu (Tibetan name) 瑪奴（藏名）

Common Name:

英文名﹕

elecampane inula root, root of elecampane inula, root of racemose inula

Distribution:

產地﹕

Mainly being produced in Hebei, also being produced in Xinjiang, Gansui, Shaanxi, Sichuan, Henan, Zhejiang provinces of China .

主産於河北，此外，新疆，甘肅，陝西，四川，河南，浙江等地亦産。

Properties

characteristics﹕

性味﹕

acrid, bitter, warm.

辛，苦，溫。

Channels meridians entered:

 

歸經﹕

liver, spleen.

肝，脾。

Medical functions:

藥理﹕

 

Effect of arresting  cough, expelling  phlegm, promoting urination, antibiotic, arresting pain, rid of parasites.

In vitro studies showed that alantolactone is toxic to white cells

止咳排痰，利尿，抑菌，鎮痛，驅蟲

體外培養試驗表明，土木香內酯為一變應原，對白細胞有毒性。

Actions & Indications:

主治﹕

Being used in bulging pain in rib cage, chronic stomachache, round worm parasites, vomiting and diarrhea, injuries in the rib cage, over active fetus, asthma, abundance of phlegm.

用於胸脅，脘腹脹痛，慢性胃炎，蛔蟲病，嘔吐瀉痢，胸脅挫傷，急性胸肋痛，胎動不安，氣喘痰多。

Daly Dosage:

每日用量﹕

3~9 g

Samples of formulae:

處方舉例﹕

藥方名：六味安消散 (中國藥典)

處方：土木香50g，大黃 200g，山奈100g，寒水石（煅）250g，訶子150g，鹼花300g。
上為細末，過篩，混勻。
功能主治：和胃健脾，導滯消積，行血止痛。主胃痛脹滿，消化不良，便秘，痛經。
用法用量：每服1.5至3g，1日2-3次。
注意：孕婦忌服。
