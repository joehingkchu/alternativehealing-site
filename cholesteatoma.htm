Cholesteatoma

By: Joe Hing Kwok Chu

A benign condition of unknown cause characterized by an expanding mass of cholesterol crystals and keratinized skin in the inner ear, in the brain, or in the spinal cord.

Symptoms include hearing loss, ear fullness and pain.
