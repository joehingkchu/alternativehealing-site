fu pen zi 覆 盆 子

by: Joe Hing kwok Chu

Present Day Applications:

現代應用﹕

 

Pharmaceutical name:英文藥名﹕

Fructus Rubi

Biological name:拉丁文學名﹕

Rubus chingii Hu

Rubus corchorifolius L

Rubus foliolosus D. Don

Pron. in Japanese:

日語發音﹕

fukubonshi

Pron. in Korean:

韓語發音﹕

pokpunja

Pron. in Cantonese:

粵音﹕

fuk pun tse
  zhong tian pao 種田泡, weng niu 翁扭, niu nai mu牛奶母。

Common Name:

英文名﹕

palmleaf raspberry fruit, Chinese raspberry, rubus

Distribution:

產地﹕
  1. Rubus chingii Hu: central, southern China
  2. Rubus corchorifolius L.: Hebei, Shaanxi
  3. Rubus foliolosus D. Don: Yunnan, Guizhou, Sichuan

 

  1. 覆盆子分布華中﹐華南各地；
  2. 懸鉤子(Rubus corchorifolius L. F.)，分布河北﹐陝西各地；
  3. 灰毛果莓(Rubus foliolosus D. Don)，分布雲南﹐貴州﹐四川各地。

Properties (characteristics)﹕

性味﹕

sweet, sour, warm

性温，味甘、酸。

Channels (meridians) entered:

歸經﹕

liver, kidney

肝,腎

Actions & Indications:用途﹕

spermatorrhea, incontinence, impotence, premature ejaculation, frequent urination.

作為強壯．強精藥用．主治遺精．遺尿．陽萎早泄 ．頻 尿。

Chemical ingredients:化學成份﹕
organic acid 有機酸
sugar 糖类
ellagic acid 逆没食子酸
β-sitosterol β-谷甾醇
   

Dosage:

用量﹕

6 ~ 12 g. in decoction

6 ~ 12 克。入煎劑。

Samples of formulae:處方 舉例﹕

wu zi yan zong wan

五子衍宗丸﹕枸杞子﹐菟絲子﹐覆盆子﹐五味子﹐車前子五味中藥组成。
