Sheltered in a circle of trees, this discreet and accommodating home turns inward to find its heart in a generous, open-plan living room with, brick hearth and fireplace, handsome glass doors to the front deck, and skylights that make design elements of the sun and the stars.

The pristine kitchen, which adjoins the living room, features a beautiful tile floor, central island with cooktop and breakfast bar, built in oven, and lots of natural light through a row of windows overlooking the beautifully landscaped garden.

A flight of stairs leads up to the huge master suite with serene and private treetop views, room-size walk-in closet, and spacious bath.

The kitchen and two of the three substantial bedrooms downstairs open onto a sweeping deck that adds room for play and for entertaining to the lush and secluded garden, where a carpet of lawn alternates with rock and redwood to create a wonderful array of natural textures.

There are one and one-half baths downstairs, and the two-car garage includes a convenient workshop. On a lovely lot of almost a quarter of an acre, this comfortable family home is only a short walk away from Portola Valley’s Ormondale and Corte Madera schools.
