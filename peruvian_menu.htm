Yellow hot - pepper powder, cheese powder, whole milk powder, ground crackers, rice starch, stabilizer, garlic powder, fine salt.
 

Paella: a typical Spanish recipe and is traditionally cooked in a "paellera" - a round flat pan with two handles - which is then put on the table. It is mixed seafood seasoned with garlic, sauteed with cilantro & base, mixed with peas and carrots then served with sarza criolla (red onions marinated in lime juice)

Carapulcra:  the oldest peruvian stew. It contains dried potatoes, chilis, peanuts, cumin and, in this case, pork (it can be chicken or both). Originally, the carapulcra was made in clay pots; this custom is still kept in Chincha, a city in Ica (south of Lima) and in some restaurants.
