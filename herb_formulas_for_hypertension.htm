Chinese herb formulae for hypertension 治血壓高藥方

 

By: Joe Hing Kwok Chu  （in English and Chinese 中英文)

 

Traditional Chinese Medicine

Hypertension can be classified into 4 categories:

  1. gan yang shang kang 肝陽上亢型

  2. yinxu with fire  陰虚火旺型

  3. gan shen yin xu (liver-kidney yin deficient) 肝腎陰虚型

  4. yin yang both deficient 陰陽兩虛型

 

和內風、blood stasis、痰阻3個兼証。

Folk Formulas

 

For treatment of neurasthenia, vertigo, sequelae from concussion, palpitation, insomnia, hypertension, cirrhosis with edema:

Prescription:

luo bu ma 3 to 9 g. Pour in boiling water and use as tea.

Do not boil the herb. (Xinjiang Chinese Herb Medicine Handbook)

 

 

治神經衰弱 ，眩暈 ，腦震盪後遺症，心悸，失眠，高血壓，

肝硬化腹水，浮腫：

羅布麻一至三錢。 開水沖泡當茶喝，不可煎煮。(《新疆中草藥手冊》)
