Transdermal Delivery Media      

by: Joe Hing Kwok Chu

 

 

Transdermal delivery media refers to substances used to enhance the penetrating speed of medicine through the skin for absorption by the body. The  substances used as transdermal delivery media should not cause serious irritation or damage to the skin.

 

 

The Chinese herbs like menthol , long nao, eucalyptus oil,  chuan xiong, xiao dou kou extract possess transdermal delivery effect.

 

 

透皮吸收促進劑（簡稱促透劑）是指可以加快藥物穿透皮膚的速度而不對皮膚形成嚴重刺激和損害的藥物。

中藥之中﹐ 薄荷腦 (薄荷醇), 龍腦, 桉樹油及川 芎, 小豆蔻 , 蛇床子提取物都具有這種作用。
