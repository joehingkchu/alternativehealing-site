jian bu wan 健步丸

Edited by: Joe Hing kwok Chu   (in English and Chinese 中英文)

 

Name of Formula: jian bu wan

Application: for treatment of qixu (qi deficient)  and xue xu (blood deficient),  achy loin and knees, weak  legs, difficult to walk.

Composition

shu di huang 8.32%，du zhong 5.56%， huang bai 5.56%，bai shao 5.56% , bu gu zhi 5.56% , niu xi 5.56% , wu wei zi 5.56% , bai zhu 5.56% ,    gui ban 5.56%, dang gui 5.56% , cang zhu 5.56%，huang qi 5.56%, shan yao  5.56% , gou qi zi 5.56%，tu si zi 5.56%，ren shen 5.56%，fen fang ji 2.76% ,  qiang huo 2.76%，fang feng 2.76%。

Grind the above herb into powder and mix evenly with cooked honey. Make into pills of the size of soy bean. Take about 8 to 10 pills each time, 3 times a day, with drinking water.

健步丸

藥方名：健步丸

主治：氣 虛 血虛 ，腰 膝酸軟，下肢痿弱，步履艱難

 

组成：

熟地黃 8.32%， 杜仲5.56%， 黃柏5.56%，白 芍5.56% , 補骨脂5.56% , 牛膝5.56% , 五味 子5.56% ,

白朮5.56% ,    龜板 5.56%, 當歸 5.56% , 蒼朮5.56%，黃 耆5.56%, 山藥 5.56% , 枸杞子5.56%，

菟絲子 5.56%，人参 5.56%， 黃耆2.76%,  粉防 己2.76% ,  羌活2.76%， 防風 2.76%。

以上各藥，研磨細末。煉蜜為丸。 每次服8～10 粒。每日三次。用開水服。
