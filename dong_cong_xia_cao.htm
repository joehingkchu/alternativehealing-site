dong chong xia cao

By: Joe Hing Kwok Chu

Pharmaceutical name:Cordyceps Sinensis
Biological name:Cordyceps sinensis (Berk.) Sacc. and the usually the larval are the remains of Hepialus varians
Pron. in Japanese:tochukaso
Pron. in Korean:tong ch'ug ha ch'o
Pron. in Cantonese:tung chung ha cho
Other Name:chong cao, dong chong cao
Common Name:cordyceps
Distribution:Sichuan, Qinghai, Guizhou, Yunnan, Tibet
Properties (characteristics)sweet, arm
Channels (meridians) entered:lung, kidney
Actions & Indications:tonifies kidney and improves yang: for impotence, chronic lower back pain, afraid of cold, over abundance of mucus and tears, from kidney yang deficiency (shenyangxu).

tonifies lung yin and kidney yang: lowering phlegm production,  and stop bleeding. (chronic cough and wheezing from deficiency, blood in phlegm from consumption.

Medical Function:antibiotic effect, inhibits contraction of smooth muscles.
Chemical ingredients:

Vitamin B12, ergosterol, cordycepic acid

Amino Acid group:

 crude protein 27.52%.  amino acid: lysine,  aspartic acid , (tian men dong suan), threonine (su an suan), taurine  (niu huang suan).

 

Dosage:4.5 to 12 g per day.
Samples of formulae:For weak old people with chronic bronchia inflammation: dong chong xia cao 10 g, kuan dong hua 6 g, sang bai pi 8 g, gan cao 3 g, xiao huai xiang 2 g, water 600 c.c.  Boil down to 200 c.c. Make into 3 servings per day.
Modern Research:to be loaded
Cautions:large doses might be toxic
