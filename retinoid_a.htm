retinoid acid

Retinoic acid (RA) is a natural acidic metabolite of vitamin A of the body. It plays a critical role in normal development, growth and differentiation of cells by modulating the expression of target genes.

It affects the growth of bones and the metabolism of the epithelium (outer skin).
