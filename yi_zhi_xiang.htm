yi zhi xiang 一枝香

by: Joe Hing kwok Chu

Pharmaceutical name:

英文藥名﹕

 Herba Vernoniae Cinereae

Botanical name:

學名﹕

 Vernonia Cinerea L.

Pron. in Japanese:

日語發音﹕

 yambaru higotai

Pron. in Korean:

韓語發音﹕

  

Pron. in Cantonese::

粵音﹕

 jat1 zi1 hoeng1

Other Names:

別名﹕

 :ye xiang niu 夜香牛，lang wei la hua 狼尾拉花，bronchitis weed 氣管炎草

Common Name:

外文名﹕

 English: ash-coloured fleabane, ash-coloured ironweed, little ironweed, purple fleabane, purple-flowered fleabane.

Vietnamese: bach dau ong

Indian: Sahdei, Sahaderi, Sandri; Kukseem, Kukur-songa (Bengal); Moti-sodori (Bombay); Sadori (Gujerat); Naichette, Mukuthipundu (Tamil), Gherittekarnina (Telagu); Pirina, Puvankurutala (Malayalam)

German: Kleines Eisenholz, Kleines Scheinaster

Distribution:

產地﹕

 Distributed in India (seen in Andhra Pradesh), Indochina, Indonesia, Africa, Japan, the island of Taiwan and mainland China, in Hunan, Sichuan, Fujian, Hubei, Yunnan, Zhejiang, Guangxi, Guangdong, Jiangxi and other provinces..

分佈於印度（見於安得拉邦）、中南半島、印度尼西亞、非洲、日本、台灣島以及中國大陸的湖南、四川、福建、湖北、雲南、浙江、廣西、廣東、江西等地。

Properties

(characteristics)﹕

性味﹕

 cool, slightly bitter.

涼，微苦。

Channels (meridians) entered:

 

歸經﹕
Medical functions:

藥理﹕

 

 Mannitol extracted from the whole herb was given orally to lab mice at 200 mg per mouse and showed that it possesses antitussive effect (Coughs were induce by delivery ammonia by steam), but it does not possess expectorant effect; but no significant expectorant effects (mouse phenol red method). Most mice with this dose resulted in diarrhea. Reducing the dose had no cough inhibiting effect, and therefore its effect on cough is not specified and should be further identified. The total glycoside does not possess cough inhibiting effect. But the aglycone possesses cough inhibiting effect.

Decoction in vitro showed no significant inhibitory effect.against Staphylococcus aureus, catarrhal aureus, Streptococcus pneumoniae.

從全草中提出的甘露醇給小鼠口服200毫克/鼠，有明顯止咳作用（氨水蒸氣引咳法）， 但無明顯的祛痰作用（小鼠酚紅法）。用此劑量多數鼠均現腹瀉，減少劑量則無止咳作用，因此，其止咳作用是否具特異性尚需進一步確定。從中提出的總 黃酮甙並無
止咳作用，但甙元則有止咳作用。

煎劑在試管內對金黃色葡萄球菌、卡他球菌、甲型鏈球菌及肺炎雙球菌無明顯的抑菌作用。

Actions & Indications:

主治﹕

 For arresting cough, transforming phlegm, pacifying asthma. Being used in chronic bronchitis.

鎮咳，化痰，平喘。治慢性氣管炎。

Chemical ingredients:

化學成份﹕

 Whole plant contains mannitol and glycosides. The aglycone of the glycoside is 6-Hydroxyluteolin

全草含甘露醇和黄酮甙。黄酮甙的甙元为6-羟基木犀草素（6-Hydroxyluteolin)

Dosage:

用量﹕

 To take orally, use 6 to 9 g. for  decoction.

内服，煎湯：6～9克。
